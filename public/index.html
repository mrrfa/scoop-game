How to play

Pip (pink): W A S D move · F make ice · G break ice

Mallow (mint): ↑ ← ↓ → move · / make ice · . break ice

Ice appears in the cell you are facing, and only on empty floor with no fruit, exit, player or enemy. Ice blocks enemies. Break it any time, so you can’t be trapped for good.

Collect every fruit to open the exit, then walk in. Touching an enemy costs a shared life. P or Esc pauses. In solo mode the arrow keys also move Pip.
